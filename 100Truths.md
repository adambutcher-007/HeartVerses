**Here is a curated list of 100 deeply meaningful truths gleaned from the ESV Bible.** These are organized thematically for clarity, drawing from core doctrines, attributes of God, the person and work of Christ, the Holy Spirit, salvation, the Christian life, and God's promises. Each is phrased as a concise truth with a key reference (often a primary or representative verse).

### **Truths About God**
1. God is sovereign over all creation and works all things according to His will. - Ephesians 1:11  
2. God created the heavens and the earth out of nothing. - Genesis 1:1  
3. God is holy and calls His people to be holy. - 1 Peter 1:15-16  
4. God is love, and love comes from Him. - 1 John 4:7-8  
5. God is merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. - Exodus 34:6  
6. God is faithful; He keeps His covenant. - Deuteronomy 7:9  
7. God never lies and His word proves true. - Titus 1:2; Proverbs 30:5  
8. God is omniscient, knowing all things. - Psalm 139:1-4  
9. God is omnipotent; nothing is too hard for Him. - Jeremiah 32:17  
10. God is just and will judge the world in righteousness. - Psalm 96:13  

### **Truths About Jesus Christ**
11. Jesus is the only way to the Father and salvation. - John 14:6  
12. Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life. - John 14:6  
13. Jesus is our High Priest who sympathizes with our weaknesses. - Hebrews 4:15  
14. Jesus is fully God and fully man. - John 1:1, 14; Colossians 2:9  
15. Jesus died for our sins and rose again for our justification. - 1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Romans 4:25  
16. Jesus is the resurrection and the life. - John 11:25  
17. Jesus gives abundant life. - John 10:10  
18. Jesus is the Good Shepherd who lays down His life for the sheep. - John 10:11  
19. Jesus is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. - John 1:29  
20. Jesus intercedes for us at the right hand of God. - Romans 8:34; Hebrews 7:25  

### **Truths About the Holy Spirit**
21. The Holy Spirit convicts the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment. - John 16:8  
22. The Holy Spirit regenerates and renews us. - Titus 3:5  
23. The Holy Spirit indwells and seals believers. - Ephesians 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:16  
24. The Holy Spirit helps us in our weakness and intercedes for us. - Romans 8:26  
25. The Holy Spirit produces fruit in believers: love, joy, peace, etc. - Galatians 5:22-23  
26. The Holy Spirit leads us into all truth. - John 16:13  
27. The Holy Spirit empowers believers for witness and service. - Acts 1:8  
28. Believers are baptized by one Spirit into one body. - 1 Corinthians 12:13  
29. The Holy Spirit gives spiritual gifts for the common good. - 1 Corinthians 12:4-7  
30. Do not grieve the Holy Spirit. - Ephesians 4:30  

### **Truths About Humanity and Sin**
31. All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God. - Romans 3:23  
32. The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life. - Romans 6:23  
33. Humans are created in the image of God. - Genesis 1:27  
34. The heart is deceitful above all things. - Jeremiah 17:9  
35. Apart from Christ, we are dead in trespasses and sins. - Ephesians 2:1  
36. No one is righteous on their own. - Romans 3:10  
37. Pride goes before destruction. - Proverbs 16:18  
38. Man does not live by bread alone but by every word from God. - Deuteronomy 8:3; Matthew 4:4  
39. We are fearfully and wonderfully made. - Psalm 139:14  
40. The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. - Proverbs 9:10  

### **Truths About Salvation**
41. Salvation is by grace through faith, not works. - Ephesians 2:8-9  
42. Salvation is found in no one else but Jesus. - Acts 4:12  
43. Whoever believes in Jesus shall not perish but have eternal life. - John 3:16  
44. Repent and believe the gospel for forgiveness. - Mark 1:15; Acts 2:38  
45. If we confess with our mouth and believe in our heart, we will be saved. - Romans 10:9  
46. God saved us according to His mercy, not our works. - Titus 3:5  
47. There is now no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus. - Romans 8:1  
48. We are justified by faith and have peace with God. - Romans 5:1  
49. Christ suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous. - 1 Peter 3:18  
50. Salvation includes regeneration by the Holy Spirit. - John 3:5-8  

### **Truths About the Christian Life**
51. Love the Lord with all your heart, soul, and mind. - Matthew 22:37  
52. Love your neighbor as yourself. - Matthew 22:39  
53. Take up your cross and follow Jesus. - Luke 9:23  
54. Be transformed by the renewal of your mind. - Romans 12:2  
55. Pray without ceasing. - 1 Thessalonians 5:17  
56. Rejoice always, give thanks in all circumstances. - 1 Thessalonians 5:16, 18  
57. Walk by the Spirit and not gratify the desires of the flesh. - Galatians 5:16  
58. Do not be anxious; make requests known to God. - Philippians 4:6-7  
59. Cast all your anxieties on Him because He cares for you. - 1 Peter 5:7  
60. Let your light shine before others. - Matthew 5:16

62. All things work together for good for those who love God. - Romans 8:28

68. Perfect peace for those whose mind is stayed on Him. - Isaiah 26:3  
69. God supplies every need according to His riches. - Philippians 4:19  
70. Nothing can separate us from the love of God in Christ. - Romans 8:38-39
